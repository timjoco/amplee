STEP 1

Create Your Band

Tap the "+" button and select "Create Band." Give your band a name and upload a photo — this becomes your band's identity on Amplee.

Once created, you land on your Band Home — your central hub for everything. Next event, setlists, roster, all right here.

STEP 2

Create Your First Event

From your band page, tap Events and hit the "+" to create a new one. Add the details — name, date, time, location.

Whether it's a gig, rehearsal, or studio session, Amplee treats them all the same: one place for all the info. Every event gets its own dedicated chat and customizable setlist. No more digging through group texts to find the address or set order. Our next email will showcase everything you can do with your first event - including the event chat, assigning a setlist, and attendance.
